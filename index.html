01 The Calling

We do not decorate.
We glorify — cloth, cut and intention, offered upward.

Glorify began in Rome in 2014, in a borrowed room beneath a shuttered chapel — a streetwear label cut between morning prayer and last light. The house rule was set early and has never moved: nothing leaves the atelier that we would not offer upward.

Every garment is cut from natural fibre, finished by hand, and tithed at the door — a tenth of every collection goes back to the streets that raised us.
